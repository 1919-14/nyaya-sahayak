The Problem

Rights that go unused, buried in paperwork.

Consumer protections, tenant rights, RTI access, welfare eligibility — citizens are entitled to them, but navigating the language and process is intimidating and scattered across PDFs, portals, and notices no one designed for a specific personal problem.

＋Legal text exists, but nobody connects it to your specific situation.

How It Works

From question to filed document.

1

Ask, in your own words

No legal vocabulary required — describe the situation as you'd tell a friend.

2

Cross-questioned, not guessed at

Low-confidence answers trigger a short clarifying question with tap-to-answer options, instead of a fabricated response.

3

Grounded, cited answer

Every claim traces to a clause — from our verified database, or a labelled live web fallback when it isn't there.

4

A document, not just advice

RTI application or complaint letter, pre-filled from the conversation and ready to send.

What Makes It Different

Built to distrust itself first.

Session memory that actually resolves "tell me more"

Follow-ups are rewritten against chat history before retrieval, so the second answer isn't the first one repeated.

Every claim is source-tiered

verified web-sourced unconfirmed

Your documents are evidence, not gospel
