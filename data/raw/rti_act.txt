SOURCE: Right to Information Act, 2005

Section 3 — Right to information.
Subject to the provisions of this Act, all citizens shall have the right to information.

Section 6(1) — Request for obtaining information.
A person who desires to obtain any information under this Act shall make a request in writing
or through electronic means to the Public Information Officer, accompanied by the prescribed
fee, specifying the particulars of the information sought.

Section 6(2) — No reason required.
An applicant making a request for information shall not be required to give any reason for
requesting the information or any other personal details, except those necessary for contacting
the applicant.

Section 7(1) — Time limit for response.
Subject to sub-section (2) of section 5 and sub-section (3) of section 6, the Public Information
Officer shall, on receipt of a request, as expeditiously as possible, and in any case within
thirty days of the receipt of the request, either provide the information on payment of such
fee as may be prescribed or reject the request.

Section 7(1) proviso — Life or liberty cases.
Where the information sought concerns the life or liberty of a person, the same shall be
provided within forty-eight hours of the receipt of the request.

Section 7(6) — Free information on delay.
If the Public Information Officer fails to give decision on the request within the period
specified, the information shall be provided free of charge.

Section 8 — Exemptions from disclosure.
Certain categories of information are exempt from disclosure, including information affecting
sovereignty and integrity of India, information forbidden by court, cabinet papers, and
information related to personal privacy that has no relationship to any public activity.

Section 19(1) — First appeal.
Any person who does not receive a decision within the time specified, or is aggrieved by a
decision of the Public Information Officer, may within thirty days from the expiry of such
period or from the receipt of such a decision, prefer an appeal to an officer senior in rank
to the Public Information Officer in each public authority.

Section 19(3) — Second appeal.
A second appeal against the decision of the first Appellate Authority shall lie within ninety
days from the date on which the decision should have been made or was actually received, with
the Central Information Commission or State Information Commission, as the case may be.

Fee structure (typical, Central Government).
Application fee is generally Rs. 10. Applicants below the poverty line are exempt from paying
any fee, subject to submission of a valid BPL certificate. Additional fee may be charged for
providing information, generally Rs. 2 per page for photocopies.
