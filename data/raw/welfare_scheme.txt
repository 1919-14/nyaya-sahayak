DOMAIN: Welfare Scheme Eligibility
SOURCE: Ayushman Bharat — Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY), illustrative summary

Overview.
PM-JAY provides a health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary
care hospitalization, to families identified based on deprivation and occupational criteria
from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) database.

Eligibility criteria (rural).
Households with no adult member between ages 16 and 59, households with no able-bodied adult
member, households with a disabled member and no able-bodied adult, landless households
deriving income from manual casual labour, and certain other deprivation categories are
automatically eligible, subject to SECC records.

Eligibility criteria (urban).
Occupational categories such as rag pickers, domestic workers, street vendors, construction
workers, sanitation workers, and similar informal-sector occupations are covered.

How to check eligibility.
Eligibility can be checked using the beneficiary's ration card number, mobile number, or
Aadhaar-linked details on the official PM-JAY portal or through empanelled hospitals'
Ayushman Mitra desks.

What is covered.
Coverage includes pre and post hospitalization expenses, medicines, diagnostics, and
transportation, at any empanelled public or private hospital, cashless and paperless.

Common reasons for rejection or gaps.
Name not appearing in SECC database, incorrect or mismatched Aadhaar/ration card details, or
residing in a state that has not fully rolled out the scheme in partnership with the central
government are common reasons a person may not find themselves covered, even if they meet the
general deprivation criteria.

Practical note.
Applicants facing rejection despite apparent eligibility can raise a grievance through the
PM-JAY grievance portal, or approach the District Grievance Nodal Officer.
